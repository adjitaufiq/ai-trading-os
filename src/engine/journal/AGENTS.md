- M6 journal (src/engine/journal) is a read-only, append-only analysis layer: unique tradeId, stored rows deep-frozen, analytics are descriptive and never change rules, params, risk or signals. Why: memory layer ≠ optimizer; history must not be rewritten.
- Journal dataset fields (assetClass, session, planned/actual entry, slippagePoints, latencyMs) are derived in fromPaperTrade from SymbolRegistry + pure resolveMarketSession + the frozen fill; FailureCategory lives in src/engine/contracts/diagnostics.ts. Why: one leaf contract avoids journal↔autopsy cycles, and M6 never reaches back into M4/M5.
