- M1B.3 (this directory) is the anti-repaint swing detector + market structure engine: input ONLY CLOSED ArenaCandles via M1B.2 calculationLockGate; output frozen SwingPoint (HIGH/LOW, strength from rejection wick) and MarketStructure (HH_HL/LH_LL/RANGE, BULLISH_MSS/BEARISH_MSS on a CLOSED close beyond the opposing confirmed swing); LEFT_BARS=RIGHT_BARS=2 (pivot high > left highs and >= right highs, mirrored for lows); no duplicates per symbol+timeframe+type+timestamp; anchors M15/H1/H4 only, M5 rejected; H4/H1 = macro bias, M15 = structure/S-R/SL anchor. Why: structure evidence must never repaint.
- Timestamp semantics: ArenaCandle.timestamp and SwingPoint.timestamp are BAR OPEN time; SwingPoint.confirmedAt is the earliest time the swing is legally observable = close of right-hand bar #rightBars = pivot.timestamp + (rightBars + 1) × timeframe (rightBars=2 ⇒ +3 × tf). Canonical: M15 pivot open 10:00 ⇒ 10:45 (never 10:30: right bar #2 is still forming and can invalidate the pivot); H1 10:00 ⇒ 13:00; H4 08:00 ⇒ 20:00. Why: look-ahead safety; regression tests in tests/confirmedAt.test.ts.
- confirmedAt must never precede completion of the last right-hand confirmation bar, and every consumer (buildStructure, priceLocationEngine, slCalculator, and any later module) must ignore a swing whose confirmedAt > asOf/now. Why: a backtest that sees unconfirmed swings looks better than reality.
