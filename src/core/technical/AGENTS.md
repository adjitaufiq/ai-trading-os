- Real-market technical slice (this directory) owns deterministic, versioned analysis policies (including the read-only reference plan), never imports src/probe, and leaves unratified thresholds RESEARCH_REQUIRED; failed observation ⇒ UNAVAILABLE/NOT_EVALUABLE/BLOCKED with no substitute data; AI WHY only receives a snapshot and returns text. Why: evidence ≠ decision, deterministic policy ≠ execution, AI ≠ authority, no mock fallback.
- Operator-style rules (trend/context/zones/EMA/patterns/regime/setup) live only in operator.ts with every numeric parameter in OPERATOR_PARAMS and every rule text in OPERATOR_RULES; any change bumps the policy version and its tests. Why: discretionary style must stay explicit, versioned and testable.
- Quick backtest (backtest.ts) replays only evaluate()/reference_plan over broker CLOSED history with visibleAt(T) filtering; gross OHLC, same-bar SL+target ⇒ AMBIGUOUS. Why: no second strategy, no lookahead, no invented spread.
- ARENA H4-primary (operator.ts ARCHITECTURE_VERSION v0.5): H4 trend (EMA21/EMA50 + structure, closed H4 bars only) is the SINGLE source of direction -> primary_bias BUY/SELL/NONE; H1 confirms, M15 setup, M5 trigger only time the H4 side; combined M5/M15 momentum NEVER picks a side — opposing local momentum sets counter_trend_condition BULLISH_PULLBACK/BEARISH_PULLBACK (warning only); H4 mixed/insufficient => NONE/WAIT; snapshot carries primary_bias/setup_bias/counter_trend_condition; opposing side is hard-blocked before order intent and again in DemoExecutionBridge as BLOCKED_COUNTER_PRIMARY_BIAS (audit: attemptedSide, primaryBias, counterTrendCondition, timeframeConflict). Why: trend-following; countertrend is risk info, never a signal.
- Reference plan, RADAR projection (RADAR_PLAN_POLICY_VERSION) and paper-open plans all use buildSidePlan in tradingState.ts; radar is dynamic until READY, paper levels freeze at open. Why: one SL/TP formula, no preview-only math, no silent mutation of open trades.
- Scalp plans anchor SL only on confirmed M15→M5 swings (SCALP_STOP_PARAMS, no H1 fallback) and cap TP2 at the nearest M15/H1/H4 opposing zone minus a buffer (TARGET_CLAMP_PARAMS); tp1/tp2 are FINAL levels, raw 1R/2R kept for audit; economics BAD_RR vetoes the actionable entry (WAIT) while the technical READY lifecycle continues. Why: scalp risk must be local and reward must respect the next wall.
- Signal safety lifecycle (deriveSignalLifecycle in tradingState.ts, SIGNAL_TTL_POLICY_VERSION/SIGNAL_TTL_PARAMS): READY freezes its origin (entry/SL/R/trigger) at birth, carries it unchanged, ends by CLOSED-M5-bar TTL, early invalidation or chasing vs frozen R, and a consumed signal_id never resurrects; backtest replays the same lifecycle and trades once per signal_id. Why: a signal is a time-bounded frozen event, never a silently moving latch.
